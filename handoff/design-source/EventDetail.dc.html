About the workshop

Power Up brought 24 students from 14 countries to Skopje for a week of lectures, lab work and field visits. Professors from FEEIT explained how a national grid balances supply and demand, and engineers from local energy companies showed what that looks like in practice.

What we did

  • A full lab day in the FEEIT high-voltage laboratory
  • A visit to a 10 MW solar plant outside Skopje
  • A team challenge: keep a simulated grid stable through a cloudy afternoon
  • An international night and a weekend in Ohrid

The workshop was organized by a team of 16 LC Skopje members. Most of them were organizing an international event for the first time.

About the lecture

Every second, the power grid has to produce exactly as much electricity as the country uses. In this evening lecture, an engineer from the national transmission operator explained how that balance is kept, what happens when a power plant suddenly trips, and why more solar power makes the job harder.

The lecture was open to all FEEIT students and was followed by questions and a short tour of the faculty's power systems lab.

About the workshop

Most AI runs in big data centres. This workshop was about the opposite: small models that run on a €10 board, with no internet connection. Participants trained models, squeezed them onto microcontrollers and built working prototypes in teams of four.

Lectures were given by FEEIT professors and engineers from local companies, with a lab day, a company visit and a day trip to Ohrid.
