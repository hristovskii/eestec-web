# Page: EESTEC Journey

**Route:** `/journey`
**Goal:** A **visual** guide showing how to get the most out of EESTEC, step by step. Minimal text, maximum visuals.

Follows the global design system in `00-overview-and-design-system.md`.

---

## Concept

A path / roadmap graphic. Desktop: horizontal or winding path; mobile: vertical path. Each step is a circle or node with an icon in `#e52a30`, a short title and one sentence. Steps animate in on scroll.

## Steps

| # | Step | One-line text (example) | Button → |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Join** | Become a member of LC Skopje. | `/join` |
| 2 | **Participate** | Attend local workshops, trainings and socials. | `/upcoming` |
| 3 | **Volunteer & Organize** | Join a team and help organize events. | `/members` (teams) |
| 4 | **Go International** | Apply for workshops and exchanges across Europe. | `/upcoming` |
| 5 | **Lead** | Become a coordinator, board member or join international teams, ECM and Congress. | `/join` |
| 6 | **Stay Connected** | Become alumni and support the next generation. | `/members` (alumni) |

## Extra elements

- On hover/tap, each step can expand to show 2–3 bullet benefits and a small photo.
- Optional quote from a member at each step (e.g. "My first exchange changed everything").
- Final call-to-action at the bottom: big **Start your journey** button → `/join`.
