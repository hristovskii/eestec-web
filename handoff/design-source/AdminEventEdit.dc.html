About the workshop

Most AI runs in big data centres. This workshop is about the opposite: small models that run on a €10 board, with no internet connection. You will train a model, squeeze it onto a microcontroller and build a working prototype with your team.

  • Lectures by FEEIT professors and engineers from local companies
  • A full lab day and a company visit
  • A team challenge and a day trip to Ohrid

No prior machine-learning experience is needed. Read the full programme.
