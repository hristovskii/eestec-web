# Section & Page: Sponsors and Partners

**Placement:** Section at the **bottom of the Home page** (directly above the footer) + optional page `/partners` ("For Companies").
**Goal:** Give visibility to sponsors and attract new companies.

Follows the global design system in `00-overview-and-design-system.md`.

---

## Home page section (required)

- Title: "Our Partners" (or "Supported by").
- Logos grouped by tier, for example: Main Partner, Gold, Silver, Partners, Media Partners, Academic Partners (e.g. FEEIT, University).
- Higher tiers get bigger logos.
- Logos in original colors on white background (or grayscale with color on hover, as decided by the board). Keep it consistent everywhere.
- Each logo links to the sponsor website (opens in new tab).
- If there are many logos: auto-scrolling carousel.
- Link at the end: "Become a partner" → `/partners`.

## "For Companies" page (`/partners`)

- Why partner with EESTEC LC Skopje (reach students of electrical engineering and computer science, employer branding, workshops, career events).
- Collaboration options / packages (short cards) + downloadable partnership offer (PDF).
- Past and current partners (full logo wall).
- Contact form for companies: company name, contact person, e-mail, phone, interest (sponsorship, workshop, job fair, other), message.

---

## Admin-managed data

- Sponsors CRUD: name, logo (upload), website, tier, active from/to dates, display order, active/inactive
- Tiers list (editable names and order)
- Partnership offer PDF upload
- Texts on the `/partners` page
- Company inquiries inbox with status
