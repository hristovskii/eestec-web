Mixing logo shapes

Logos are sized to the same visual area, not the same height, so wide wordmarks don't overpower square or tall marks. Size = √(area × aspect ratio), capped at 64% of the cell height and the cell width. The board only uploads the file; the site reads its proportions.

✕ Same height (avoid)The wordmark fills the cell and looks three times bigger than the others.
✓ Equal visual area (use)All three feel the same size. Red dashed lines show each logo's box.

Logo uploads: SVG preferred, or PNG with a transparent background, trimmed to the logo with no extra padding.

More logos than fit in one row

On Home, a tier that doesn't fit in one row (desktop: more than 6, mobile: more than 2) becomes a slowly scrolling row with faded edges, like “Partners” on the Home frames.
