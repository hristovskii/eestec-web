# Page: Contact

**Route:** `/contact` (contact info also appears in the global footer)
**Goal:** Make it easy for students, companies and other committees to reach LC Skopje.

Follows the global design system in `00-overview-and-design-system.md`.

---

## Sections

- **Contact form**: name, e-mail, subject (general, membership, partnership, media, other committee), message, spam protection. Each subject can be routed to a different e-mail address.
- **Contact info**: main e-mail, position e-mails of the board, address (FEEIT, Skopje), office/meeting room.
- **Map**: location of the faculty.
- **Social media**: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn (icons in `#e52a30`).
- **Legal info** of the association: full registered name, registration number, tax number, bank account (as decided by the board).

---

## Admin-managed data

- All contact details, social links, legal info
- Subject list and the e-mail address each subject is routed to
- Inbox of contact messages with status (new / answered / archived)
