About

What is EESTEC?

EESTEC, the Electrical Engineering STudents' European assoCiation, is a non-profit, non-political student association founded in 1986 in the Netherlands.

Today it is a network of Local Committees at technical universities across Europe. Together they organize workshops, exchanges and trainings that help students grow professionally and personally — and make friends in every corner of the continent.

Visit eestec.net

Workshops

A week of lectures, labs and city life on a technical topic, hosted by one committee for students from all over Europe.

Exchanges

Short, culture-first trips to another committee's city. The easiest way to go international for the first time.

Motivational Weekends

Weekend trainings that turn new members into confident organizers and team players.

Soft Skills trainings

Communication, leadership and teamwork sessions led by EESTEC's own certified trainers.

Competitions

Team challenges like the EESTech Challenge and RoboMac: local rounds first, international finals next.

Congress

The yearly gathering where delegates from every committee shape the future of the association.
