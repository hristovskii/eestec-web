# Page: How to Become an EESTECer & Rules (Join Us)

**Route:** `/join`
**Goal:** Explain clearly who can join, how to join, what the rules are, and provide the membership application form.

Follows the global design system in `00-overview-and-design-system.md`.

---

## Sections

### 1. Why join?
- 4–6 benefit cards with icons: international experience, technical knowledge, soft skills, friends across Europe, organizing experience, contacts with companies.

### 2. Who can join?
- Eligibility requirements (e.g. students of FEEIT and other faculties; exact rules defined by the board).

### 3. How to join: steps
- Numbered visual steps, e.g.:
  1. Fill in the application form below
  2. Come to a weekly meeting / info session
  3. Join a team
  4. Become a full member (after the conditions defined by the board)
- Membership fee (if any) and how to pay.

### 4. Rules & code of conduct
- Members' rights and obligations
- Code of conduct (respect, inclusion, representing LC Skopje and EESTEC properly)
- Rules for applying to international events (e.g. motivation letter, obligations after the event, report or Memories post). Exact rules are defined by the board.
- Link / download of official documents (statute, internal rules) as PDF.

### 5. FAQ
- Accordion, e.g.: "Do I have to study at FEEIT?", "How much time does it take?", "Does it cost anything?", "Do I need to speak English?", "Can I join in my final year?"

### 6. Membership application form
- Name, e-mail, phone, faculty, study program, year of study, interests (multi-select: IT, PR, design, events, fundraising, trainings), "How did you hear about us?", consent checkbox.
- Confirmation message and confirmation e-mail.
- Link to `/journey` for the visual overview.

---

## Admin-managed data

- All section texts, benefit cards, steps, rules (rich text), FAQ items (CRUD, reorder)
- Uploaded documents (PDF)
- Membership applications list: status (new / contacted / accepted / rejected), export to CSV/Excel
- Membership fee info
